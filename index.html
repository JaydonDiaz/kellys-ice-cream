Our Sweet Story

From a tiny kitchen to your neighborhood.

Hi, I'm Kelly! For as long as I can remember, I've had a passion for ice cream. Though I later became a nurse by trade, I've never known a time when ice cream wasn't the center of my day.

Together with my husband, our inaugural batch of Kelly's ice cream was born in the tiny kitchen of our first home. We carted our frozen favorites to local farmer's markets, slowly introducing our newest flavors to anyone willing to try a scoop.

"We just knew we were doing what we loved."
