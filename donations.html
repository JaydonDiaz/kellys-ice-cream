Give Back

Donation requests.

Thank you for thinking of Kelly's Homemade Ice Cream. We are committed to supporting organizations in our community through in-kind donations and fundraising events. Our mission is simple: to support the local community through exceptional products, steadfast customer service, and uncompromised integrity.

Before You Apply

What we can and can't support.

We genuinely care and carefully consider every request, though we're not able to accommodate them all. Please give us at least two weeks — ideally a month before your event.

Our Focus

  • Children
  • Education
  • Police, Fire, Medical
  • Non-Profits

What Does Not Apply

  • Organizations that discriminate based on race, color, gender, religion, sexual orientation, national origin, age, or disability
  • Political, legislative, or lobbying organizations
  • Individuals (scholarships, stipends, fellowships, personal assistance)
Time Needed

Please complete the form below and give us no less than two weeks to process your request — at least one month before your event is preferred.
