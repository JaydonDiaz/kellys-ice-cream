Our Blog

Stories from behind the counter.

Flavor drops, family traditions, and community events — a look at what's happening at Kelly's, straight from Kelly.

February 6, 2024 · Stores

International Eat Ice Cream for Breakfast

We celebrate Eat Ice Cream for Breakfast on February 18th from 9am–2pm, when 50% of sales benefit Orlando Health Arnold Palmer Hospital for Children. The day was created in memory of Malia Grace, whose family wanted to honor her love of ice cream and her fight against pediatric cancer. Every scoop — and every donation — makes a difference.

October 15, 2023 · Family

Traditions

It's a tradition in our family to have our kids wait until their first birthday for their first scoop of ice cream! Having the ice cream truck come out for our children's birthdays is a fun tradition — watching their faces light up over that first bite brings us so much joy. An ice cream social is such a fun way to bring people together to celebrate.

August 10, 2023 · Flavors

Back to School!

For this month's Featured Flavors, we brought back flavors that remind us of waking up for school — like our Cosmic Brownie-inspired creation, plus fan favorites Breakfast Buddies and Kit Kat. Breakfast Buddies is a labor of love: we steep Cap'n Crunch & Corn Pops into our cream base, then layer in homemade Fruity Pebble marshmallow treats.

May 12, 2023 · Family

Makin' Memories With Mom

One of the best ways to celebrate motherhood is to make memories together. One way Kelly loves to spend time with her family is making ice cream sandwiches — just cut the side of a pint of homemade ice cream and press it between your favorite cookies. Don't forget to take photos; ice cream brings families together.

April 7, 2023 · Local Orlando

407 Day

407 Day celebrates Orlando's local restaurant scene, and Kelly took us on a tour of her favorite spots — Jeff's Bagel Run, Buttermilk Bakery, Swine and Sons, and Antonella's Pizzeria. Our Winter Park Social House Scoop Shop even serves an Oatmeal Cream Pie Ice Cream inspired by the Swine and Sons recipe next door!
